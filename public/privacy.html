Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 9, 2026

Welcome to GBP Auto Master ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy governs the privacy policies and practices of our website, located at gbpautomaster.in, and the GBP Auto Master software application.

1. Information We Collect

When you use our service, we collect the following types of information:

  • Personal Information: Name, email address, and profile picture provided through Google OAuth during registration.
  • Google Business Profile Data: Business locations, reviews, ratings, and metrics associated with your Google Business Profile account.
  • Usage Data: Information on how you interact with our application.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect primarily to provide, maintain, and improve our services. Specifically, we use your data to:

  • Connect and sync your Google Business Profiles to our dashboard.
  • Monitor new reviews left by your customers.
  • Utilize artificial intelligence (Google Gemini) to generate contextual, SEO-optimized replies to your customer reviews.
  • Post those replies on your behalf back to your Google Business Profile.
  • Process subscription payments (handled securely via Razorpay).

3. Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance

GBP Auto Master's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Specifically:

  • Data Access: We strictly access only the data necessary to provide our core automated review response and calendar scheduling services.
  • No Third-Party Sharing: We do NOT sell, transfer, or share your Google user data with unauthorized third parties. Data is only shared with trusted LLM providers (e.g., Google Gemini API) strictly for the purpose of generating review replies.
  • Data Deletion: You may revoke access to your Google account at any time via your Google Account Permissions page, and you may request full deletion of your data from our servers.

4. Data Storage and Security

We implement industry-standard security measures, including Row Level Security (RLS) on our databases, to ensure your data is protected against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. We utilize Supabase as our secure database provider.

5. AI Processing and Content Generation

When our system generates an automated reply, the text of your customer's review is sent to our AI model (Google Gemini). This data is transmitted securely and is strictly used to generate the reply text. It is not used to train global AI models.
